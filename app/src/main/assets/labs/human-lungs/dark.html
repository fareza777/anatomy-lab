How it works

CO₂ comes from alveolar ventilation and a resting CO₂ output; alveolar O₂ from the alveolar gas equation; saturation from the Severinghaus fit of the dissociation curve; pressure falls with an 8.4 km scale height. The body's automatic responses (it would breathe harder by itself at altitude) are left out so you can see each effect.
