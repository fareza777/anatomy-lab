How it works

Throughput uses a scanner limit proportional to EUV source power plus a fixed back-end limit, and yield uses Murphy's model with an illustrative 1.2 cm² critical area plus an overlay penalty. Real fabs have hundreds of tools, re-entrant flows, queueing and binning; the scene shows one of each stage and eight hoist vehicles, not the thousands of moves per hour of a real fab.
