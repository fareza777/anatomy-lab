How it works

The flight is a point-mass simulation over a round Earth with an exponential atmosphere, drag, thrust that rises as the air thins, and a fixed pitch programme. Stage masses and engines are similar to a modern medium-lift launcher but not a model of a specific vehicle; real guidance optimises the path for each payload.
