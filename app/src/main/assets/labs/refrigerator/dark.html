How it works

A single-temperature thermal model with a fixed wall conductance, door leak and cooling capacity; the COP is 45 % of the Carnot value between evaporator and condenser temperatures. Fridge time runs 90× faster than real time. The freezer is assumed to sit about 22 °C below the fridge.
