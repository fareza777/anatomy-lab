How it works

A lumped-parameter teaching model, not an operator-training simulator. Point kinetics with one delayed-neutron group uses the prompt-jump approximation; fuel, coolant, steam-generator and steam-pressure dynamics are single nodes; xenon is time-lapsed about 240× so an hours-long transient fits in half a minute; decay heat is a simplified 40 s decay. Real PWRs have many more loops, protection channels and spatial effects. Values are typical of a large four-loop PWR, not a specific plant.
