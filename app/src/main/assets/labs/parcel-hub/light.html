How it works

Stage capacities are simple linear approximations: induction grows with the AMR fleet, the sorter with belt speed, and the chutes and outbound dock are fixed limits. The animation shows 40 carriages and 7 chutes slowed by roughly 20×; a real hub can run hundreds of chutes, several sorters and thousands of robots, with queueing, blocking and failures that this model does not capture.
