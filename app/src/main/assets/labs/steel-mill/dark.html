How it works

A lumped capacity model of an integrated mill, not a heat-and-mass balance. Tonnages, temperatures and casting speeds are representative; real plants depend on ore chemistry, furnace campaign, refractory wear, product mix and logistics. The animation is stylised and slowed so the ladle, strand and strip can be followed.
