How it works

A continuous queueing approximation of a discrete-event terminal, not a model of a specific port. Crane cycles, AGV trips and yard cycles are typical values; real terminals vary with ship layout, weather, labour, customs and yard design. The animation is slowed and stylised so the individual machines can be followed.
