How it works

A lumped, deliberately legible model of a complex plasma. Fusion reactivity uses a Gaussian stand-in for the D-T cross-section; confinement, density limits, quench and disruption thresholds are representative rather than predictive. Real tokamaks need profile control, edge-localised modes, runaway-electron mitigation and a tritium breeding blanket; this lab shows the couplings, not a reactor design.
