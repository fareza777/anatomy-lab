How it works

A lumped, continuous model of a closed cabin, not a flight-qualified ECLSS simulation. Volumes, leak rates, scrubber kinetics and water recovery are exaggerated so the drifts are visible in minutes rather than days. Real stations use redundant loops, batteries, pressure-control assemblies and resupply; the chemistry and the direction of every coupling here are representative.
