How it works

The field follows the rigid-body relation B = E/(0.3 R) with a 2.8 km bending radius; luminosity is a simplified scaling with bunch number and β*, and the stored energy is scaled from the real 6.8 TeV fill. The ring is drawn with 20 magnet and cavity stations instead of more than 1,600 magnets, and the beams are slowed by about 20× so they can be followed. The detector is a schematic slice, not a specific experiment.
