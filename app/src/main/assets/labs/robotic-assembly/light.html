How it works

A lumped takt-and-cycle model, not a discrete-event simulation of a real body shop. Weld counts, robot times and changeover penalties are representative values; actual lines vary with body design, weld schedules, fixture changes and buffer sizes. The animation is slowed so the robots and the indexing conveyor can be followed.
