How it works

A point-mass energy model with a circular-loop force estimate and a simple capacity cap, not a full dynamic simulation. Real coasters use clothoid loops, finite train length, wheel friction, air drag and block sections. Heights, speeds and g-forces here are representative; the animation is stylised and not to scale.
