Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Anatomy Lab: How Things Work ("the app") is made by F7 Developer. This policy explains what data the app and the services it uses handle, and the choices you have.

Data the app itself collects

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. All interactive systems are bundled inside the app and work offline. Your settings (theme, haptics), saved systems and recently opened systems are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Android's backup may include these settings in your own Google account backup if you have backup turned on.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you buy "Remove Ads", the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The app receives only a confirmation that the purchase belongs to your Google account; it never sees your payment details.

Sharing

When you choose "Share" or "Share as poster", the app creates an image or text on your device and hands it to the Android share sheet. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose an app to send it with.

Children

The app is educational and suitable for a general audience. It is not directed primarily at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

The app requests only the Internet and network-state permissions, which are needed for ads and purchases. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to F7 Developer through the developer contact address on the app's Google Play listing.
